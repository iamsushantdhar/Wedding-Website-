Our story

Sushant and Aarushi met in school, more years ago than either of them likes to admit — somewhere between forgotten homework and long walks home. They still can't agree on who made the first move, only that once it was made, there was no turning back.

Since then: oceans crossed, distances closed, and every challenge along the way met with the same quiet answer — each other.

Now begins the next chapter — one home, one family, and a lifetime of the ordinary, extraordinary days that follow “happily ever after.”
